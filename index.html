Colour that grows out gracefully.

We specialise in complex colour fades, foils, custom blondes and balayage. The word comes from the French balayer, “to sweep”: colour painted on by hand for a soft gradient that looks natural.

It is flattering, glossy and low-maintenance, and it suits just about everyone. Not sure how it will look on you? Book a colour consultation and talk it through first.
